            FFXIV Timer v0.10.0

            Add to calendar

            A calendar file (.ics, the standard iCalendar format) with the times you choose. This page makes the file itself: nothing is sent anywhere, and nothing you choose here is saved.

            What

            Good to know

            • Each time is its own entry, written in UTC, so your calendar shows it in your time zone, before and after a daylight-saving change.
            • A weather entry lasts until the weather changes again, a transition covers its one weather period, a node covers its window, and a reset is a 15-minute block at the moment it happens.
            • Something already under way, such as a node that is up now, starts at the moment you make the file and covers the rest. Nothing starts in the past, and a weather change or reset that has already happened is left out.
            • Weather and node times are calculated from the game's data and not yet compared with the game itself; each entry says so. A reset time that rests on community sources only says "time unverified" in its title.
            • The file does not update itself. After a patch, download a new one; entries you imported before stay in your calendar until you delete them. Importing a file again may add a second copy, depending on your calendar app.
            • Whether a file's alarm is used depends on your calendar app; some use their own reminder instead. An entry already under way has no alarm.
            • A file holds at most 500 entries. Above 200, this dialog asks you to check the count first.
